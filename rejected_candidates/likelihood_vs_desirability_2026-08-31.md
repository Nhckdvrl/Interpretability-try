# Rejected Candidate — Likelihood ≠ Desirability / Valence

Date: 2026-08-31
Status: **TERMINAL REJECTION**

## Natural question

> When estimating whether an outcome will happen, does a model keep probability separate from whether that outcome is desirable, or does positive/negative valence systematically pull the forecast?

The intended scientific object was a possible probability-versus-value factorization rather than generic forecasting calibration.

## Why it looked good

- Probability and desirability are independently meaningful quantities in ordinary decision making.
- A forecast should not become more likely merely because an outcome is attractive.
- Real-world forecasting could provide objective resolution gold rather than researcher labels.
- A mechanistic account could in principle distinguish evidence accumulation from a value/valence writer that contaminates probability readout.

## Kill evidence

The exact behavioral object is already directly occupied by 2026 work.

`OptimismBench: Forecasting Bias and the Alignment Effect in Language Model Judgment` explicitly defines **valence-dependent probability distortion**, retains the sign of probability skew, decomposes it into valence components, and studies the effect as a consequence of alignment using base-versus-chat causal comparisons. This is not generic calibration work: the probability–valence interaction is the named phenomenon.

Related 2025–2026 forecasting and probability work further crowds probability distortion, nonlinear probability weighting, calibration, and real-world forecasting. Therefore reframing the same object as `probability vs desirability`, moving to prediction-market questions, or adding mechanistic probes would be a direct successor to an already named phenomenon.

## Death code

`KILL-N0/N1 / DIRECT-VALENCE-PROBABILITY-DISTORTION-MOTHER`

## Nearest-neighbor warning

Do not resurrect as:

- optimism bias;
- wishful thinking;
- good outcome vs likely outcome;
- valence vs probability;
- utility/value contamination of forecasting;
- positive vs negative event probability;
- another prediction-market or forecasting dataset;
- another model, prompt, language, calibration method, probe, SAE, patching or steering approach.

## Resurrection condition

Only reconsider if a qualitatively distinct natural phenomenon is found where desirability is not the title-level source of probability distortion and where the new scientific claim cannot be reduced to valence-dependent forecasting bias or generic probability calibration.
