# Rejection — Event Completion / Imperfective Paradox

Date: 2026-09-01  
Verdict: **KILL-NOVELTY / KILL-DATA**

## Natural question

When an event is described as ongoing, does the model distinguish an event that has begun from one whose intended endpoint was actually reached?

## Semantic aliases

- imperfective paradox
- culmination / telicity
- process vs result state
- event completion
- attempted vs completed event

## Why it looked promising

The distinction is ordinary and important: `was building a house` does not entail that the house was completed. It appeared to offer a clean semantic axis with process/result representations and causal-use tests.

## Decisive kill evidence

ACL 2026 already studies the imperfective paradox in LLMs, including a teleological-bias interpretation and internal representational analyses of process versus result information. More seriously, a 2026-08-25 follow-up argues that the original benchmark itself is substantially mis-specified and reconstructs lexically matched minimal pairs, reframing the apparent phenomenon.

Thus both the scientific object and its internal-representation direction are already occupied, while the most obvious substrate is unstable.
