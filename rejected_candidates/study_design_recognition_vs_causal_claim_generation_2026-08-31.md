# Rejected Candidate — Study-Design Recognition ≠ Causal-Claim Generation

Date: 2026-08-31
Status: **TERMINAL REJECTION**

## Natural question

> Can a model correctly recognize that a study design supports association rather than causation, yet still turn the result into an unhedged causal claim when it summarizes or explains the study?

The tempting mechanistic formulation was an epistemic-evidence state that fails to constrain a downstream causal-language writer.

## Why it looked good

- The evidence boundary is externally defined by study design rather than by an LLM judge.
- Cross-sectional/observational evidence versus causal inference is a real scientific distinction with high practical importance.
- The same article can in principle support a clean within-item test of methodological recognition and spontaneous generation.
- A strong dissociation could distinguish failure to understand evidence from failure to route understood evidence into language generation.

## Kill evidence

The behavioral object is already directly owned by very recent mother work.

Nature Human Behaviour 2026 `Quantifying the prevalence and impact of overreaching causal claims in social science` explicitly tests LLM-generated summaries of cross-sectional/associational research and finds that models introduce or strengthen causal claims beyond the underlying evidence. It studies multiple prompts, multiple leading models, abstracts and full text, and shows that methodological/caution interventions sharply reduce but do not fully remove causal overstatement.

ACL 2026 `Narrative License and Model Sycophancy in LLM Summaries of Scientific Work` independently makes rhetorical amplification in scientific summaries a title-level object and explicitly includes **causal overreach** and rhetorical confidence among its core dimensions.

Therefore adding a recognition probe/task and asking where the causal-language writer ignores that recognition would be a direct mother-behavior → mechanism extension, not a new scientific object. It is also structurally adjacent to the already crowded generic cognition/knowledge → action/generation gap.

## Death code

`KILL-N0 / DIRECT-CAUSAL-OVERREACH-SUMMARIZATION-MOTHER`

## Nearest-neighbor warning

Do not resurrect as:

- correlation vs causation in summaries;
- methodological knowledge vs generated claim;
- evidence strength vs rhetorical confidence;
- hedge preservation vs causal language;
- abstract vs full-text causal calibration;
- scientific papers from another discipline;
- another open model or careful prompt;
- mechanistic localization of a `causal-language writer`.

These remain inside the causal-overreach / narrative-license scientific family.

## Resurrection condition

Only reconsider a different epistemic-routing phenomenon if its title-level object is not scientific summarization, causal overclaiming, hedge loss, narrative license, or generic knowing-to-generation consistency, and if the new behavior exists independently on current open models.
