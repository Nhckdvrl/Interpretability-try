# Literal semantics vs pragmatic enrichment architecture

Date: 2026-09-01  
Verdict: **KILL-NOVELTY**

## Semantic aliases

- literal-first vs direct pragmatic integration

## Natural question considered

When literal truth conditions conflict with a conversationally plausible inference, does an LLM first construct a stable literal-semantic representation that is later overridden by a pragmatic channel, or does context alter interpretation from the outset so literal and pragmatic content are not causally separable?

This is a real psycholinguistic/pragmatic debate independent of any benchmark: literal-first/modular accounts versus direct-access, constraint-based, or parallel pragmatic integration.

## Why it initially looked viable

ACL Findings 2026 PaCE provides >3,000 manually verified context-flip items across implicature, speech acts, presupposition, and deixis, with systematic literal-vs-pragmatic conflicts across nine models. The headline phenomenon is broad and naturally relevant to reliable language understanding.

## Decisive N2 kill

The PaCE mother does not merely establish the behavioral Context Sensitivity Gap. It explicitly claims that models possess **suppressed literal understanding capabilities/pathways**, interprets pragmatic hallucination through Dual-Process Theory as a fast System-1 pragmatic/lexical heuristic that can be overridden by slower System-2 reasoning, and argues that lexical/pragmatic priors override context/logical truth. Strict prompting and CoT are presented as evidence that the literal capability is latent but behaviorally locked out.

Therefore the proposed mechanistic alternatives are not an omitted theory-level axis. The mother has already selected the `literal capability exists + pragmatic prior suppresses/overrides it` interpretation. A causal activation-patching paper would most naturally be described as internal verification of that headline interpretation.

Under the current N2 delta-width rule, behavioral evidence being weaker than causal MI does not reopen the concept-level question.

## Nearest-neighbor warning

Do not resurrect by renaming the two pathways `semantic/pragmatic`, `literal/intent`, `System 2/System 1`, `truth-conditional/conversational`, or by replacing strict prompting with probes/patching. The conceptual factorization is already explicitly owned by PaCE. A 2026 activation-bottleneck paper on pragmatic constraint reasoning further crowds the general `knowledge present but not routed` mechanism family.

## Resurrection condition

Only reopen if a genuinely wider independent scientific question spans multiple pragmatic phenomena/windows and is not reducible to PaCE's suppressed-literal-pathway / heuristic-override interpretation; the new axis must remain meaningful even if PaCE is removed entirely.
