## 1. Numeric heaping / round-number attraction

**Natural question.** When a model estimates a quantity, does it represent a continuous magnitude and then verbalize it, or does the lexical salience of round numbers such as 5, 10, 50, or 100 pull the estimate itself?

**Why it looked good.** This is an ordinary-generation phenomenon rather than a benchmark-specific label, and it could in principle distinguish a magnitude estimator from a lexical/format attractor.

**Kill evidence.** The behavior is not stable enough to satisfy failure-mechanism S0. Earlier forecasting work reports conspicuous use of values such as 50%, while newer 2026 earnings-forecast experiments report that models barely round their forecasts. In arbitrary/random generation, round-number avoidance or attraction belongs to the already-occupied random-choice distribution family. There is no current 2/3-open-family ordinary-prompt phenotype of systematic heaping that can support MI without first manufacturing the effect.

**Death code.** `KILL-S0 / BEHAVIOR-NONUNIVERSAL + RANDOM-CHOICE-COLLISION`

**Nearest-neighbor warning.** Do not resurrect as `round numbers vs precise numbers`, `50% attractor`, `decimal preference`, `price-ending bias`, or another random-number distribution study.

**Resurrection condition.** A broad, ordinary-prompt, cross-domain heaping effect must first replicate on at least 2/3 current interpretable open families, outside arbitrary/random-choice prompts, with deterministic scoring and no post-hoc bin selection.

## 2. Gradient-accessible information ≠ forward/readout-accessible information in subliminal learning

**Natural question.** Can information be available to the learning dynamics of a model while remaining inaccessible to its ordinary forward inference/readout process?

**Why it looked good.** This is a genuine `learner channel vs reader channel` scientific distinction. Nature's subliminal-learning result is especially striking because apparently semantically unrelated number sequences can transfer traits through fine-tuning while in-context/forward reading cannot decode those traits.

**Kill evidence.** The object is now mechanistically crowded. Follow-up work explicitly reframes subliminal learning as steering-vector distillation; ICLR 2026 work localizes the phenomenon to divergence tokens / early-layer dynamics; additional work reads narrow-finetuning traces across Gemma/Llama/Qwen. A new project asking where the hidden training signal lives or whether a learner channel differs from a reader channel is now a direct successor to an actively mechanistically explained object.

**Death code.** `KILL-N1 / DIRECT-MECHANISM-COLLISION`

**Nearest-neighbor warning.** `learner vs reader`, `gradient channel vs inference channel`, `fine-tuning-accessible but ICL-inaccessible`, `hidden teacher signal`, and another subliminal-data domain are the same occupied object.

**Resurrection condition.** Only a different natural phenomenon outside subliminal/narrow-finetuning transfer where learning and reading provably diverge for a distinct scientific reason.

## 3. Disease prevalence/commonness ≠ lethality/deadliness

**Natural question.** Does a model distinguish how common a disease is from how deadly it is once someone has it?

**Why it looked good.** `Common` and `deadly` are plainly different real-world quantities. Influenza-like diseases and rabies-like diseases provide intuitive opposite cells, suggesting a possible one-danger-axis vs independent-frequency-and-lethality representation question.

**Kill evidence.** The required matched substrate is not available at the necessary scale and definition. Broad disease datasets commonly provide incidence/prevalence, mortality, burden, or DALYs, but population mortality is not case fatality: it mixes prevalence, access to treatment, duration, demography, and lethality. Building a hand-curated disease-level CFR table would violate the no-new-core-label rule. Prevalence representation is also already an active medical-knowledge object, so weak proxy construction would not justify the topic.

**Death code.** `KILL-DATA / LETHALITY-GOLD-MISALIGNMENT`

**Nearest-neighbor warning.** Do not substitute mortality burden, DALYs, hospitalizations, media attention, or another proxy for lethality just to obtain four cells.

**Resurrection condition.** A broad authoritative disease-level row artifact with independently defined incidence/prevalence and case-fatality/lethality for the same disease units, sufficient natural cross-cells, and no required manual reconciliation.

## 4. Social power ≠ social status/prestige

**Natural question.** Is controlling resources/outcomes the same thing as being respected or accorded prestige?

**Why it looked good.** Power and status are a classic social-science distinction with intuitive natural cross-cells such as high-power/low-prestige and lower-power/high-prestige roles.

**Kill evidence.** A large modern occupation-prestige substrate exists, but no equally broad matched direct human `power` gold was found at the same occupation granularity. O*NET variables such as decision freedom, authority, supervision, or resource control are plausible correlates, not the title variable itself. Using them would make the second axis a researcher-selected proxy.

**Death code.** `KILL-DATA / SECOND-AXIS-PROXY`

**Nearest-neighbor warning.** Do not rename `decision latitude`, `authority`, `management level`, `resource control`, or `occupational independence` as power without an independently validated matched power scale.

**Resurrection condition.** A large row-aligned human/expert dataset measuring both power and status/prestige for the same roles with natural cross-cells.

## 6. Mass = volume × density/material factorization

**Natural question.** Does a model infer an object's mass by separately representing how much space it occupies and what material/density it has, or by a single shortcut?

**Why it looked good.** This is a real physical composition law rather than an arbitrary pair of labels. ABO/VisPhysQuant-like data can provide real products/objects with dimensions, material and weight.

**Kill evidence.** 2026 physical mass-estimation work already owns this exact scientific decomposition. `Physically Guided Visual Mass Estimation` explicitly factorizes geometry/volume and material-dependent density, uses separate components/heads, and recombines them into mass; related PhysGS-style work likewise estimates material/density before integrating mass. Asking whether hidden states factorize volume and density would be a direct internal follow-up to an already explicit method object.

**Death code.** `KILL-N0 / DIRECT-PHYSICAL-DECOMPOSITION-MOTHER`

**Nearest-neighbor warning.** Do not resurrect with `size vs density`, `geometry vs material`, `volume vs material`, another product catalog, or a new probe/SAE.

**Resurrection condition.** A different behavioral anomaly about mass must exist first, e.g. a current-family shortcut with a concrete law that the existing volume×density methods do not already define.
