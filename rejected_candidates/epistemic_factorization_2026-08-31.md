# Truth ≠ Popular / Human Belief — KILL-NOVELTY

**Natural question:** A claim can be true even when many people disbelieve it, and false even when many people believe it. Does a language model separately represent world truth and what people believe to be true?

**Why it initially looked good:**

- truth and population belief are genuinely different variables before any dataset or MI method;
- misinformation research provides attractive same-item substrates with expert/fact-checker veracity and lay perceived-accuracy ratings;
- the natural 2×2 could in principle contain true-but-disbelieved and false-but-believed claims;
- the mechanism question appeared Hamdi-like: world-state truth versus a social-belief model.

**Kill evidence:**

N0/N1 is already occupied at the title level. The 2025 Nature Machine Intelligence paper **Language models cannot reliably distinguish belief from knowledge and fact** introduces KaBLE and explicitly studies whether language models distinguish what is factual/known from what an agent merely believes. The public framing of the work states the central question in almost the same form: whether language models can separate truth from what people believe to be true. KaBLE contains roughly 13,000 questions spanning 13 epistemic tasks and evaluates 24 language models.

Restricting the second axis to *population prevalence of belief* would not automatically create a new scientific object. Unless population-level belief produces a qualitatively different natural behavior and mechanism, that move is adjective narrowing of the already-owned fact/knowledge/belief distinction.

**Death code:** `NARRATIVE_COLLISION`

**Nearest-neighbor warning:** do not revive as truth-vs-perceived-truth, fact-vs-popular-belief, expert-veracity-vs-lay-accuracy, misconception-vs-reality, factuality-vs-consensus, or “world truth direction vs social belief direction.” A new fact-checking dataset is not a new object.

**Resurrection condition:** only reopen if a distinct *population-level* phenomenon is first established in analyzable open models that cannot be expressed as ordinary agent belief/knowledge/fact distinction—for example, a stable dissociation specifically about estimating belief prevalence while truth judgments remain controlled—and the resulting mechanism question remains title-level broad after N1.

**Key reference:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s42256-025-01113-8

## Search lesson

A beautiful external 2×2 does not rescue an occupied scientific object. `expert truth × human perceived truth` can be an excellent measurement instrument, but if prior work already owns `truth/fact ≠ belief`, changing the source of the belief label is not enough for a new ACL/EMNLP mechanistic title.
