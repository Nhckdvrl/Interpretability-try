# Rejection — Speech-Act Pragmatic Intent Representation

Date: 2026-09-01  
Verdict: **KILL-NOVELTY**

## Natural question

Does a model distinguish what an utterance is grammatically shaped like from what social action the speaker is performing, such as asserting, requesting, promising, or predicting?

## Semantic aliases

- speech act
- communicative intent
- pragmatic intent
- assertion vs request
- promise / commissive / prediction

## Why it looked promising

Form and intent can cross: a declarative may function as a request and an interrogative may not simply be a question. That gives natural matched cells and a potentially reusable pragmatic-state object.

## Decisive kill evidence

LREC 2026 `The Emergence of the Pragmatic Dimension in Instructed-LMs` directly crosses declarative/interrogative form with assertive/request communicative intent and analyzes hidden states in Qwen2.5, Gemma3, and Llama3.2. Its central result is a representation shift from syntactic form toward pragmatic intent under instruction tuning.

The core form-vs-intent representation object is therefore already owned. Switching to promise, prediction, or another subtype would mainly change the speech-act label while keeping the same scientific axis.

## Strongest-neighbor warning

Do not revive as assertion/request direction, promise/prediction vector, commissive latent state, or speech-act steering unless a genuinely orthogonal scientific object is introduced.

## Death code

`F2 / N0-N2 — direct modern-open hidden-state ownership of syntax vs pragmatic intent.`

## Resurrection condition

A new question must be conceptually independent of communicative-intent representation, not merely a finer speech-act taxonomy or stronger causal method.
