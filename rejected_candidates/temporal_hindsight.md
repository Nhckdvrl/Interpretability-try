## 2026-08-27 phenomenon-first round

### Parametric hindsight in historical decision tasks

**Natural phenomenon:** Hindsight bias is the distortion of an earlier judgment by knowledge of what happened later.

**Why it initially looked promising:** `HindsightBench` (2026) reports strong date-triggered leakage of realized outcomes into historical financial decisions and a striking generation effect, including a within-vendor switch from Qwen3 to Qwen3.6. The phenomenon is natural and the audit is cheap.

**Kill evidence:** The paper explicitly states that the existence of the effect is already settled and positions its contribution as an audit protocol, then supplies the decisive date-manipulation matrix, memory probes, effective-cutoff estimation, and serving-condition analysis. A new project on generic parametric hindsight would therefore begin after the mother phenomenon has already been carefully isolated and operationalized. More importantly, its strongest reported effect is generation-specific: absent across the tested 2024 open-weight generation and present in 2026-generation models, which makes it a poor fit for a broad cross-family mechanistic mother question unless we first establish a common phenomenon on the exact locally analyzable checkpoints.

**Death code:** `NARRATIVE_COLLISION`

**Resurrection condition:** A distinct temporal phenomenon with broad same-generation open-weight replication and an unoccupied mechanism question, not merely another leakage audit or training-cutoff probe.

**Reference:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18867
