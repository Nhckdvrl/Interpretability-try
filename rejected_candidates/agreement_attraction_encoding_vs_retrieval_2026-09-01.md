# Agreement attraction: encoding distortion vs retrieval interference

Date: 2026-09-01  
Verdict: **KILL-BEHAVIOR (for current PASS-REGISTER standard)**

## Semantic aliases

- agreement attraction encoding-vs-retrieval locus
- feature distortion vs cue-based misretrieval
- subject-number corruption vs distractor retrieval
- agreement interference architecture

## Natural question

When agreement attraction occurs, is the subject representation already distorted during encoding/maintenance, or does the correct representation remain intact until a distractor wins cue-based retrieval at the verb?

The question is genuinely paper-scale and comes from a mature psycholinguistic debate.

## Decisive kill evidence

The registration premise is not yet strong enough on >=2 relevant modern open-model families under the same theory-diagnostic attraction design.

- The strongest 2026 comprehensive transformer study uses classic Wagers et al. agreement-attraction factorial materials, but the current modern family in its core open-model panel is Gemma-3; the other families are GPT/GPT-2, BLOOM, and XGLM. It also emphasizes substantial cross-model divergence, especially outside the PP configuration.
- ACL 2026 provides modern Llama/Mistral/Gemma causal evidence for syntactic agreement units, but does not establish the agreement-attraction phenotype or encoding-vs-retrieval contrast.
- The broader ACL 2026 comprehension battery establishes similarity-based NP interference on Llama-3.2/Qwen3/Gemma3, but that is not the same agreement-attraction factorial phenotype.

Promoting this candidate would therefore require running Llama/Qwen first to discover whether the exact Wagers-style diagnostic premise exists. That violates the current rule that GPU cannot be used to discover what the paper is about.

## Nearest-neighbor warning

Human work in 2018–2026 already strongly develops representation-distortion, retrieval-interference, and hybrid accounts. A future LLM project must not merely map these labels onto layers; it needs causal predictions that separate corrupted target representations from intact-target/misretrieval states.

## Resurrection condition

Reopen only if a public paper/artifact establishes the same qualitative agreement-attraction factorial signature on at least one additional genuinely modern open family (e.g. Llama-3.x/Qwen3/Mistral-current) with row-level stimuli/scoring, while no new LLM MI work has already causally adjudicated encoding distortion vs retrieval competition.
