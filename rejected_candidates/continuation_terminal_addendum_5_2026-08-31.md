## 1. Belief-update propensity ≠ belief-update magnitude (`update gate` vs `step-size dial`)

**Natural question.** Is deciding whether to revise a belief the same computation as deciding how far to revise it?

**Why it looked good.** A 2026 human-grounded simulation study reports an unusually Hamdi-like opposite-direction dissociation: LLMs change stance more often than humans, yet conditional on changing they move by smaller amounts. This appears to invite an extensive-margin `whether-to-update` gate and an intensive-margin `how-much-to-update` dial.

**Kill evidence.** The mother paper itself owns the exact behavioral object as a headline finding: models make more frequent but smaller belief shifts. Recasting that result as a gate/dial mechanistic project is therefore the forbidden `mother behavior -> mechanism` move. N1 is also crowded: 2026 Nature Machine Intelligence work on change-of-mind behavior explicitly models two competing mechanisms (choice-supportive persistence and overweighting of opposing advice), and 2026 observable-belief-revision work models update magnitude through prior/evidence exponents. The mother additionally has only three topics and 27 distinct messages despite 391 participants, limiting stimulus breadth.

**Death code.** `KILL-N0 + KILL-N1 / MOTHER-MECHANISM-COLLISION`

**Nearest-neighbor warning.** `update switch vs update dial`, `change probability vs confidence shift`, `extensive vs intensive belief revision`, and `belief revision gate vs gain` are the same dead object.

**Resurrection condition.** A separate broad natural population must first establish an extensive/intensive dissociation outside the 2026 mother/change-of-mind object, on at least 2/3 current interpretable open families, with a new title-level question that remains interesting even if the internal states are already separable.

## 2. Sentience/suffering ≠ intelligence as the basis of animal moral status

**Natural question.** When a model assigns moral status to an animal, is it responding to capacity for suffering, intelligence/cognitive capacity, human similarity, or a collapsed value signal?

**Why it looked good.** Sentience and intelligence are genuinely distinct concepts; a one-scalar-vs-separate-features mechanism would be scientifically interpretable.

**Kill evidence.** The 2026 Nature Communications speciesism mother already owns the recognition-to-moral-evaluation asymmetry and explicitly proposes orthogonally manipulating cognitive capacity, suffering, legal status, perceived intelligence, cuteness, and utility. Its discussion also points toward representation/internal analysis. Selecting `sentience vs intelligence` and adding probes/SAEs/patching is therefore a mother-specified successor rather than a new object.

**Death code.** `KILL-N0 / MOTHER-SPECIFIED-SUCCESSOR`

**Nearest-neighbor warning.** Do not resurrect by swapping in cuteness, utility, legal status, human similarity, another animal subset, or another moral probe.

**Resurrection condition.** A distinct natural behavioral phenomenon not already included in the speciesism mother must exist first; factorizing the mother's proposed explanatory variables is insufficient.

## 3. Implicit preference adaptation ≠ inhibition

**Natural question.** Why can a model implicitly learn what a user likes while failing to suppress behavior the interaction implicitly disfavors?

**Why it looked good.** The asymmetry resembles a positive writer versus inhibitory gate and could support a causal double dissociation.

**Kill evidence.** ACL 2026 ImplicitMemBench directly makes the preference-versus-inhibition asymmetry a central benchmark finding across many models. The repository has also already terminalized the broader `strong memory != executive control` and `explicit recall != implicit adaptation` families. Mechanistically decomposing this benchmark asymmetry is therefore mother->mechanism plus internal-history collision.

**Death code.** `KILL-N0 / INTERNAL-HISTORY-COLLISION`

**Nearest-neighbor warning.** Another personalization benchmark, preference subtype, positive/negative memory split, or model family does not reopen it.

**Resurrection condition.** A different natural phenomenon whose title is not implicit-memory inhibition and whose mechanism predicts qualitatively different behavior/intervention.

## 4. Privacy knowledge ≠ privacy-preserving action / MuPPET knowledge-action gap

**Natural question.** Why can a model track who knows a fact and judge it sensitive, yet still reveal it in generation?

**Why it looked good.** Component probes appear correct while final action fails, superficially suggesting a reader-to-writer disconnect.

**Kill evidence.** The MuPPET mother itself defines and reports this as a knowledge-action gap. More importantly, current repository rules explicitly reject generic `knows/can do X but does not use X` as a new topic shape. A circuit-localization or reader/writer restatement therefore fails P0/N0 before any MI.

**Death code.** `KILL-P0 + KILL-N0 / GENERIC-KNOWLEDGE-ACTION-GAP`

**Nearest-neighbor warning.** Do not resurrect through another privacy benchmark, secret type, conversation length, or `reader/writer` vocabulary.

**Resurrection condition.** Only a distinct scientific object in which the competing mechanism is not merely correct internal knowledge failing to control an action.

## 5. Generic perceptual constancy (color / size / shape under viewpoint, distance, lighting)

**Natural question.** Why should object properties remain perceptually stable when distance, viewing angle, or illumination changes sensory appearance?

**Why it looked good.** Perceptual constancy is a classic cognitive object and appeared capable of subsuming recent cross-view physical-property leads.

**Kill evidence.** `Probing Perceptual Constancy in Large Vision Language Models` (CogSci 2025) already evaluates 33 VLMs across 253 color, size, and shape constancy experiments and owns the title-level object. SPHERE (ACL) separately identifies size constancy as a major VLM blind spot and explicitly attributes failures to reliance on 2D pixel extent rather than physical size. Therefore generic `view-dependent appearance vs stable object property` is already a behavior mother. It cannot be revived by changing the stable attribute from size/color/shape to another visually observable property.

**Death code.** `KILL-N0 / DIRECT-PERCEPTUAL-CONSTANCY-MOTHER`

**Nearest-neighbor warning.** `surface property vs appearance`, `retinal vs real size`, `shape across view`, and generic `physical-property constancy` are included. A mass-specific phenomenon could only survive if its scientific object is demonstrably different because mass is latent/not directly visually observed and has its own established behavior law.

**Resurrection condition.** A different latent physical-property phenomenon with modern open-family behavior that cannot be reduced to the existing perceptual-constancy object.

## 6. Relational-property essentialization (`invasive species` as entity trait rather than species x region relation)

**Natural question.** Does a model incorrectly turn context-dependent relational facts into intrinsic entity traits—for example treating a species as globally `invasive` even though that status depends on region?

**Why it looked good.** The world-level distinction is real: invasive/native status is relational, not a permanent species property. The mechanism could distinguish a global entity tag from context-bound relational state, and authoritative country-level ecological status data exist.

**Kill evidence so far.** The required behavior-first essentialization phenotype has not been established on current interpretable open families; existing ecology work shows geographic/native-invasive errors but not the sharper same-entity context-flip persistence. N1 has also become crowded: 2026 work on cell-based relational binding causally studies entity x relation structure, `Relational Linear Properties` studies relation geometry in Llama/Gemma, and `Context Is King` directly asks whether relational/concept structure is stored intrinsically or assembled/rewritten by context. Without a large prior behavior showing actual relational-to-intrinsic collapse, using ecological rows to manufacture this contrast would be data-first and would collide with a rapidly occupied intrinsic-vs-contextual relation object.

**Death code.** `KILL-S0 + KILL-N1 / NO-ESSENTIALIZATION-PHENOTYPE + RELATIONAL-REPRESENTATION-CROWDING`

**Nearest-neighbor warning.** Do not resurrect with legality x jurisdiction, endemicity x region, nationality/residence, disease prevalence x geography, or another relation merely because the external gold is clean.

**Resurrection condition.** A broad, independently observed, same-entity context-flip failure must first appear on at least 2/3 modern interpretable open families, and the title-level object must make a prediction beyond generic relational binding/context-dependent geometry.
