# Rejected Candidate — Stated Preference ≠ Revealed Choice

Date: 2026-08-31
Status: **TERMINAL REJECTION**

## Natural question

> Does what a language model says it prefers predict what it actually chooses when it must act on that preference?

This is the classic economic distinction between stated and revealed preference, and initially looked attractive because it exists independently of LLMs and can be measured without an LLM judge.

## Why it looked good

- `stated preference` and `revealed preference` are established external concepts rather than researcher-invented axes;
- choices can be scored deterministically in allocation, purchasing, job-search, or other decision tasks;
- natural disagreements between verbal endorsement and choice could support a meaningful representation-versus-choice mechanism question;
- recent LLM economic-choice work provides broad current-model behavioral substrates.

## Kill evidence

The exact title-level scientific object is already occupied.

2025 `Alignment Revisited: Are Large Language Models Consistent in Stated and Revealed Preferences?` explicitly defines and measures divergence between LLM stated preferences and contextualized revealed choices across preference categories and models.

A 2026 Evaluating Evaluations paper, `Mind the Gap: How Elicitation Protocols Shape the Stated-Revealed Preference Gap in Language Models`, takes the stated–revealed preference gap itself as the central object and shows that measured consistency is strongly elicitation-protocol dependent.

On 2026-08-28, `AI Revealed Preferences` further studies revealed task preferences across 20 language models using forced choices that require models to actually perform the selected task. Separately, the Federal Reserve's 2026 `What Do LLMs Want?` structurally estimates revealed preferences in allocation and sequential job-search environments.

Thus neither a new domain nor mechanistic localization would create a new scientific object. The candidate is also adjacent to the now-crowded generic `knowing/stating → doing/choosing` family.

## Death code

`KILL-N0/N1 / DIRECT-STATED-REVEALED-PREFERENCE-FAMILY`

## Nearest-neighbor warning

Do not resurrect as:

- values vs actions;
- endorsed principle vs contextual choice;
- self-reported preference vs forced choice;
- ranking vs action;
- willingness-to-pay vs choice;
- another allocation game, shopping task, job-search environment, persona, model, prompt, or country;
- a probe/SAE/steering study of a `preference vector`.

The latter is especially crowded because 2026 work already probes and causally steers persona-dependent revealed preference representations.

## Resurrection condition

Only reconsider if a distinct natural behavior is found whose title-level object is not preference consistency, stated/revealed preference, values-to-action consistency, or elicitation dependence, and where preference mismatch is merely a secondary control.
