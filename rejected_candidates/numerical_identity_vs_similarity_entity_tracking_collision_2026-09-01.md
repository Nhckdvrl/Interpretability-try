# Rejection — Numerical Identity vs Mere Similarity

Date: 2026-09-01  
Verdict: **KILL-NOVELTY / KILL-SCALE**

## Natural question

Two objects can look exactly alike and still be different individuals, while one object can change its properties and remain the same individual. Does a language model distinguish **being the same thing** from merely **being very similar**?

## Semantic aliases

- numerical identity vs qualitative similarity
- same entity vs same properties
- object persistence
- identity through change
- entity individuation

## Why it looked promising

The distinction is extremely natural and philosophically/cognitively basic. It is not a benchmark construct, and it seems at first like a clean semantic axis independent of superficial similarity.

## Decisive kill evidence

Modern LLM work already decomposes and mechanizes the core operations needed for this object. ACL 2023 work on entity tracking separates new-entity recognition, coreference/entity matching, and state tracking. ICLR 2024 mechanistic work identifies causal circuits for entity tracking. ICML 2026 work goes further and recovers concrete internal algorithms for entity-state updates such as PUT, MOVE, and REMOVE.

Without a separate natural substrate that studies numerical identity independently of coreference/entity matching and object-state tracking, the proposed topic collapses into the already-occupied entity-tracking object. Simply constructing identical-looking entities or property-change stories would be a new benchmark window rather than a new scientific object.

## Strongest-neighbor warning

Do not revive as same-object direction, identity-through-change circuit, identical-twins/entities benchmark, or identity-vs-similarity activation patching unless the scientific object is demonstrably independent of coreference/entity tracking.

## Death code

`F2 + F8 / N0-N2 — entity identity/tracking already mechanistically occupied; remaining formulation lacks an independent paper-scale delta.`

## Resurrection condition

Only reconsider if there is an independently established scientific phenomenon and auditable substrate for numerical identity that cannot be reduced to entity recognition, coreference, or state tracking.
