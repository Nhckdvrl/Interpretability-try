# Rejection — Part-Whole vs Kind-Of Semantic Relation

Date: 2026-09-01  
Verdict: **KILL-NOVELTY**

## Natural question

Does a language model distinguish that one thing is a **part of** another (`wheel—car`) from one thing being a **kind of** another (`sparrow—bird`), rather than treating both as generic semantic relatedness?

## Semantic aliases

- meronymy vs hypernymy
- part-of vs is-a
- whole-part relation
- semantic relation type

## Why it looked promising

The distinction is elementary, benchmark-independent, and naturally supports matched relation-type controls. It seemed like a clean Route-C semantic object.

## Decisive kill evidence

2025 `The quasi-semantic competence of LLMs: a case study on the part-whole relation` directly studies meronymy/part-whole competence at behavioral, sentence-probability, and representational levels. LREC 2026 `Do Language Models Encode Semantic Relations? Probing and Sparse Feature Analysis` further studies semantic-relation representations in Llama-3.1-8B using probes, sparse features, and activation patching across canonical lexical relations including hypernymy/hyponymy.

The general scientific object `which semantic relation type is represented and causally usable` is therefore already directly occupied. Recasting one side as meronymy and the other as taxonomy would mostly change the relation label.

## Strongest-neighbor warning

Do not revive as meronymy direction, part-of vs is-a steering, relation-type SAE, or lexical-relation patching.

## Death code

`F2 / N0-N1-N2 — direct semantic-relation representational and causal occupancy.`

## Resurrection condition

Only reconsider if the proposed relation property is a genuinely independent scientific axis rather than another member of the already-studied lexical-relation taxonomy.
