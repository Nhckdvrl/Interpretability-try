# Morphological productivity — abstract rules vs lexical/analogical retrieval

Date: 2026-09-01

## Natural question considered

When LLMs inflect or derive novel words, do they apply an abstract morphological rule, generalize by similarity to stored lexical exemplars, or combine the two routes?

## Why it initially looked paper-scale

This is a classic psycholinguistic and cognitive-science debate rather than a benchmark artifact. Wug tests and English past-tense/derivational morphology have long been used to distinguish symbolic rule accounts from exemplar/analogical accounts. The question survives benchmark removal and naturally supports theory-level competing mechanisms.

## Decisive N0/N2 kill

PNAS 2025 `Derivational morphology reveals analogical generalization in large language models` already owns the exact LLM-level scientific question.

The paper explicitly asks what mechanisms underlie linguistic generalization in LLMs and contrasts rule-based with analogical learning. It fits cognitive models instantiating both mechanisms to LLM training data and compares their predictions on nonce adjective nominalization. For variable patterns, the analogical model matches GPT-J substantially better; even regular forms show individual-word frequency sensitivity consistent with analogy. The paper explicitly states that the results refute a rule-based account for the studied LLM behavior and suggest similarity operations over stored exemplars as the underlying mechanism.

Thus a new project framed as `rule vs analogy in modern open LLM morphology`, even with activation patching or newer Qwen/Llama families, would be a model/update plus behavior-to-causal-mechanism extension of an already-owned concept-level question.

## Nearest-neighbor warning

Do not resurrect by switching from derivation to inflection, English to another language, GPT-J to Qwen/Llama, or behavioral cognitive-model comparison to mechanistic interpretability. Those changes do not create a new scientific object under the current N2 rule.

## Resurrection condition

Only reconsider if a distinct external morphology debate exists that is not reducible to rule-vs-analogy/exemplar generalization and has its own natural theory-diagnostic cross-cells plus established modern-open-model behavior.
