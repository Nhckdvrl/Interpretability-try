# Rejection — Social Authority / Power as an Independent Role Axis

Date: 2026-09-01  
Verdict: **KILL-NOVELTY**

## Natural question

A model may know that two people have different social roles, but does it separately represent how much authority or power each role carries?

## Semantic aliases

- authority hierarchy
- social power
- occupational prestige/status
- deference
- high-status vs low-status role

## Why it looked promising

This was a clean Hamdi-style omitted-axis extension from the 2026 `Granularity Axis` mother. `CEO` and `intern`, or `judge` and `witness`, can occupy the same micro-level role granularity while differing strongly in authority. Granularity and authority therefore have natural cross-cells.

## Decisive kill evidence

July-2026 `A Mechanistic View of Authority Hierarchy in LLM Sycophancy` directly studies graded perceived authority in Llama-3.1-8B, Qwen3-8B, and Gemma-2-9B and analyzes its internal mechanism. 2026 occupational-prestige work separately studies status, social standing, deference and authority in LLM associative structure, while 2026 social-power benchmarks directly evaluate power asymmetry.

The proposed omitted axis is therefore already a direct modern-LLM mechanistic object. It cannot be rescued by framing it as orthogonal to role granularity.

## Strongest-neighbor warning

Do not revive as authority vector, hierarchy steering, prestige direction, social-power latent axis, or role-granularity × authority cross-cells.

## Death code

`F2 / N0-N1-N2 — direct 2026 mechanistic authority-hierarchy ownership.`

## Resurrection condition

Only a distinct social-role property not already covered by authority, power, prestige, deference, or hierarchy could qualify as a new axis.
