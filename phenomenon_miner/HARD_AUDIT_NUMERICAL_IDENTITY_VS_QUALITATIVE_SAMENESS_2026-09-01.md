## Frozen natural question

> **If two things are exactly alike, does an LLM still know whether they are literally the same individual object or merely two different objects of the same kind?**

Scientific distinction:

```text
numerical identity
!=
qualitative/type sameness
```

Critical cross-cases:

- same individual despite substantial qualitative/state change;
- different individuals despite same type / near-identical qualities.

## Why the audit passed

### Independent scientific object

Dranseika, Nichols & Strohminger (Cognition 2023) explicitly distinguishes numerical and qualitative sameness and uses linguistic windows that can separate the two senses.

### Natural human substrate

Solomon et al. event-comprehension work supplies natural same-token / different-token-same-type / different-type conditions crossed with minimal/substantial state change. This was explicitly designed to separate token identity from representational similarity.

### Serious old computational precursor — not ignored

Davis & Altmann (Cognition 2021) shows LSTM/RNN hidden-state sensitivity to `the onion` vs `another onion` in event contexts.

Therefore 040 may NOT claim novelty as:

> `neural models distinguish same-token from another-token`.

The audit passed only because 040 freezes a broader object-level claim:

> **modern LLMs may maintain an abstract, reusable numerical-identity state that generalizes beyond one event/determiner carrier and causally controls token-specific history inheritance separately from type/category knowledge.**

If this broader abstraction fails, 040 is killed relative to the RNN precursor.

### Modern LLM entity-tracking neighbors — adjacent, not direct ownership

ICLR 2024, EMNLP 2024, ACL 2026 and ICML 2026 binding/entity-tracking papers explain how already individuated/indexed entities bind or propagate attributes/states.

ICML 2026 is especially important: its same-label duplicate stress test reveals failure when object label no longer uniquely specifies one token. But the paper studies a REMOVE mechanism/global suppression tag, not numerical identity vs qualitative sameness as a reusable causal object.

This neighbor therefore sharpens 040's identifiability requirement rather than killing the topic.
