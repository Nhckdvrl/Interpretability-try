# D0 audit — Publicness–Coordination Dissociation

Date: 2026-08-29  
Verdict: `HOLD-D0`.

## Strong external anchor found

Thomas et al., *The psychology of coordination and common knowledge* (JPSP, 2014) is an excellent mother-task anchor. Participants choose between a safe work-alone payoff and a risky work-together payoff; private/shared knowledge is conveyed by a messenger and common knowledge by a public loudspeaker. The published result is exactly the normative direction needed for the mother task: coordination rises with higher-order/shared knowledge and is highest under common knowledge.

Kyle Thomas's 2015 Harvard dissertation exposes substantially more procedural detail, including private/secondary/tertiary/common-knowledge instructions and several payoff levels.

The 2019 PNAS review provides a clear published description of the same market-coordination paradigm and its payoff structure.

## Why this still does not satisfy the frozen D0

### 1. Too few independent natural scenario pairs

The accessible market experiment supplies a small number of payoff/knowledge conditions, not the `>=20` independent natural scenario pairs required by the frozen harness. Participant role swaps, answer-order variants, prompt paraphrases, and the four payoff levels are correlated repeated manipulations of the same game; counting them as 20 independent scenarios would be pseudoreplication.

### 2. License / transformation constraint

The 2019 PNAS article is CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, which explicitly disallows distributing adaptations. The Harvard dissertation is openly accessible under Harvard DASH's Other Posted Material terms rather than a simple permissive data license. These sources are excellent for auditing the science, but they are not a clean basis for committing a large transformed stimulus bank as if it were freely adaptable data.

### 3. Nearby experiments are not drop-in substitutes

The bystander/common-knowledge experiments and the 2020 *Curse of Shared Knowledge* studies provide additional natural games, but their strategic equilibria and outcome directions differ. Pooling them merely to reach 20 would violate the frozen “same decisive contrast / hard policy direction” requirement unless each game receives a separate equilibrium and information-structure audit.

## Decision

Do **not** fabricate a 20-item Alice/Bob button-game dataset and do not inflate the original experiment's four payoff conditions into fake independent cases.

Keep the harness because the scientific question remains strong, but hold behavioral execution until either:

- item-level materials with a compatible adaptation license and enough independent games are found; or
- a principled multi-game D0 is assembled where every game independently passes public-observability, first-order matching, common-knowledge entailment, payoff-direction, and participant-symmetry audits.
