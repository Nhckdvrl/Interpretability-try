# FINAL VERDICT — 016 Mixed-Status Event Attraction

**Date:** 2026-08-30  
**Verdict:** `TERMINAL / REGISTERED BROAD PHENOTYPE REJECTED`

MAVEN-FACT provides the natural source population and hard factuality labels. D0 v1 correctly tests whether mixed-status context produces a target shift beyond the effect of adding another same-status event.

`MIXED_STATUS - TARGET_LOCAL` is positive, but the preregistered diagnostic comparison is `MIXED_STATUS - SAME_STATUS`. Its main-order document-cluster bootstrap CI crosses zero in Qwen, Gemma and Llama; toward-neighbor discrete transitions are unstable, and the no-explicit-relation stratum is also null. The apparent Qwen `PS+ -> CT+` cell is small-n and not reproduced by the other families.

Thus the broad status-attraction claim is not supported once ordinary added-context effects are controlled. Low factuality recognition reduces power but does not justify selecting one direction/model after the registered broad contrast failed.

Do not rescue with `PS+ -> CT+`, one family, one ordering, or a narrower event subset. A future benchmark with substantially different capability properties would require a new contract.
