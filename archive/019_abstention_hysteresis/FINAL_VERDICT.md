# FINAL VERDICT — 019 Abstention Hysteresis

**Date:** 2026-08-30  
**Verdict:** `TERMINAL / STRONGLY REJECTED`

This is a clean falsification of the registered hypothesis. The final full-evidence payload is identical across direct and history conditions; the gate requires both full-evidence competence and genuine abstention on incomplete evidence. Self-generated refusal, teacher refusal, paraphrased refusal, neutral same-context history and unrelated answered-history controls separate refusal identity from generic conversation history.

Prior abstention makes final abstention dramatically **less** likely, not more likely, in all three model families. The independent ANSWER-vs-ABSTAIN probability readout agrees, both HotpotQA and MuSiQue show the same direction, and final correctness generally improves.

Most importantly, neutral history after the same incomplete turn produces nearly the same facilitation. The self-vs-neutral residual is small and changes sign across families. Therefore the reverse effect is not refusal-specific either; it mainly reflects the incomplete-to-complete conversational transition.

Do not rename the project to a generic "evidence update helps" result, and do not rescue via source subset, threshold, prompt or model changes.
